Getting started

Getting started
How does automatic trip tracking work?
MileScope uses background location (and motion signals on iOS where available) to detect drives and store them in on-device SQLite. Grant Location (Always) so trips can be recorded when the app is not open.
Billing
How do I restore Premium?
There is no MileScope account. Open the paywall and tap Restore Purchases while signed into the same App Store account that subscribed.

Gig work and taxes

Gig work
Can I use MileScope for rideshare or delivery driving?
Yes. Log your drives, classify work-related miles as business (or the category that fits), and export PDF or CSV for your own records. Confirm classification rules with a tax professional.
Taxes
Are the deduction figures tax advice?
No. Tax figures in MileScope are records and estimates for your bookkeeping. They are not tax advice and do not guarantee any tax outcome.
Taxes
Which IRS mileage rate does MileScope use?
MileScope can apply IRS standard mileage rates for the trip date (including mid-year changes) or a custom rate you set. Confirm current IRS rates when you file.

Privacy

Privacy
Is my data stored on my phone or in the cloud?
Trip data is stored locally in SQLite by default. Optional private iCloud / CloudKit backup is available on iOS if you enable it. There is no MileScope account cloud for your log.
Privacy
How do I delete all data?
Open Settings, tap Delete all data, and confirm. Export first if you need a copy. See the Privacy Policy for details.
