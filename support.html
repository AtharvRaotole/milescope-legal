Getting started

How do I start tracking trips?

Grant Location (Always) and, on iOS, Motion permission if prompted. MileScope detects drives in the background when permissions allow. Take a short test drive to confirm.

Why does MileScope ask for Always location?

Automatic mileage logging needs background location so trips can be recorded when the app is not open.

Trips and reports

A trip was missed. What do I do?

Add it manually from the trips flow. Common causes: location not set to Always, Low Power Mode, very short drives, airplane mode, or the phone powered off.

How do I export my log?

Use Reports, pick a date range, and export PDF or CSV. Share via the system share sheet.

Are the numbers tax advice?

No. Figures are records and estimates for your bookkeeping. They are not tax advice and do not guarantee any tax outcome. Review your log and talk to a tax professional when needed.

Subscription and billing

How much does MileScope cost?

$4.99 per month or $29.99 per year, with a 14-day free trial when offered. See the paywall and App Store product page for the current offer.

How do I cancel?

Cancel in Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions > MileScope at least 24 hours before renewal to avoid the next charge.

How do I restore a purchase?

There is no MileScope login. On the paywall, tap Restore Purchases while signed into the same store account that subscribed.

Can I get a refund?

Ask Apple under their refund policies. If you need a pointer to the store tools, reach us at the support page. We cannot issue store refunds directly.

Privacy and data

Where is my data stored?

In on-device SQLite by default. On iOS you can optionally enable private iCloud / CloudKit backup. There is no MileScope cloud account for trip logs.

Do you sell my data?

No. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I delete everything?

Settings > Delete all data, then confirm. You can choose to remove the iCloud copy when applicable. Export first if you need a copy.

Troubleshooting

  1. Confirm Location is Always (and Precise on iOS when available)
  2. Confirm Motion & Fitness access for MileScope on iOS
  3. Turn off Low Power Mode while testing
  4. Restart the phone
  5. Write to us at the support page with app version, device, and OS (not location coordinates)
