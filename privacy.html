Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

Operator: Atharv Raotole (individual). Contact: the support page.

This Privacy Policy describes what MileScope does with information on your device. MileScope is a mileage tracking app. There is no MileScope account and no MileScope backend that stores your trip log.

Plain summary

  • Trip, vehicle, and odometer data live in an on-device SQLite database.
  • Background location is used only to detect and record drives for mileage logging.
  • That location data stays on your device unless you export it or turn on optional iCloud backup.
  • Purchases go through Apple (and RevenueCat for entitlement checks). MileScope does not store card numbers.
  • On iOS, maps use Apple Maps (MapKit). Reverse geocoding on iOS uses Apple (via Expo Location / CLGeocoder). The production iOS build does not call the Google Geocoding API and does not embed a Google Maps API key for geocoding.
  • No selling of personal data. No ads. No third-party advertising or analytics trackers in the app.
  • Tax figures in the app are records and estimates for your own bookkeeping. They are not tax advice.

Who operates MileScope

MileScope is operated by Atharv Raotole, an individual developer in the United States. Contact: the support page.

No MileScope account

You do not create a MileScope username or password. The app works from data stored on your phone (and, if you choose, in your private iCloud). Signing in to the App Store or using your Apple ID for purchases and optional iCloud backup is handled by Apple, not by a MileScope login system.

Information stored on your device

MileScope stores the following locally in SQLite (and related on-device preferences):

Trips

  • Start and end coordinates, times, and distance
  • Optional route points along a drive
  • Addresses derived from reverse geocoding
  • Classification (business, personal, medical, charity, or unclassified, as the app supports)
  • Optional purpose and notes you type
  • Links to a vehicle when applicable

Vehicles and odometer

  • Vehicle name and optional make, model, year
  • Odometer readings you enter

Other local data

  • Frequent locations and auto-classification rules you set
  • Settings (units, rates, tracking preferences, notifications, theme, work schedule, and similar)
  • Optional profile fields you choose to enter (such as a display name)
  • Local subscription status cached so the app knows whether Premium is active
  • Demo or sample trips if you use demo mode
  • Shift Mode records: start and end times, the gig apps you pick, and any pay or tips you enter
  • Possible missed drives you mark as not a drive

Optional Timeline import files you choose are read on this device to rebuild past drives. Those files are not uploaded to MileScope.

Core tracking and reporting work offline from this local database.

Reminders and alerts, such as trip ended and the Still on shift check, are local notifications scheduled on your phone. MileScope does not use remote push notifications, so no device token or notification content is sent to a server.

Location and background tracking

With your permission, MileScope uses precise location (and, on iOS, related motion signals where available) to detect when a drive starts and stops, and to record mileage automatically, including when the app is in the background or not open (when you grant Always / background location).

Location is collected for automatic mileage logging. It is stored on device as part of your trip records. It is not sent to MileScope servers. MileScope does not operate a server that receives your live location stream.

You can revoke location permission in system Settings. Without the required permissions, automatic tracking will not work; you can still add trips manually.

When data can leave the device

Export

You export a report (for example PDF or CSV) or share a backup file through the system share sheet. You choose where that file goes.

Optional iCloud / CloudKit backup

On iOS, you may enable backup to your private iCloud (CloudKit). That copy is associated with your Apple ID, not with a MileScope account. If you keep logs on the phone only, trip data is not pushed to iCloud by MileScope. When backup is on, it includes your shifts and the pay you entered, so they come back with your trips.

Maps and reverse geocoding (iOS production build)

  • Maps: Apple Maps (MapKit). Map display may request map tiles from Apple.
  • Reverse geocoding and forward address lookup: Expo Location on iOS uses Apple's geocoder. Coordinates or address text are sent to Apple for that lookup. Results are cached on device.
  • The iOS geocoder path does not use the Google Geocoding HTTP API. A Google Maps key is not used for reverse geocoding on iOS.

Purchases

Apple processes payment. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, App Store receipt / entitlement signals, platform, and app version so Premium can unlock. MileScope does not collect or store your credit card number.

Support email

If you send a message to the support page, you send whatever you put in that message (the in-app mail templates include app version, device, and OS; they are written not to include location).

App Store updates and Expo over-the-air updates

App Store binary updates are delivered by Apple. Expo Updates is present in the project but production app.json sets updates.enabled to false and checkAutomatically to NEVER, so the shipped binary does not contact Expo's update service. Trip GPS data is never part of an update check.

Third parties that receive data (named)

  • Apple: coordinates / address text for geocoding; map tile requests; payment and App Store account activity; optional CloudKit trip backups you enable.
  • RevenueCat: anonymous app user ID, App Store receipts / entitlement status, platform, app version.

Google is not a third-party recipient on the production iOS geocoding path. Expo Updates is disabled in the shipped config, so Expo does not receive update-check traffic from the production binary.

Purchases and receipts

Subscriptions are sold through the Apple App Store. Payment method and card details are handled by Apple. RevenueCat validates entitlements and receipts so the app can unlock Premium. MileScope does not store card numbers on device as payment card data, and does not run its own card vault.

Ads, tracking, and sale of data

  • MileScope does not show third-party ads.
  • MileScope does not use third-party advertising identifiers for ads or cross-app tracking.
  • MileScope does not sell your personal data.
  • MileScope does not sell your location data.

How we use information

On-device data is used to log and display trips, calculate mileage and deduction-style figures, show maps, classify trips, build exports you request, and remember settings and Premium status.

If you import a Timeline export file, MileScope reads that file on your iPhone only and does not upload it. Widget and Live Activity snapshots may use an App Group on the device so Lock Screen and Home Screen surfaces can show your miles without a MileScope server. Siri and Shortcuts requests to start or end a shift are handled on your iPhone through the same App Group.

Tax-related numbers in the app are records and estimates for your own use. They are not tax advice. MileScope does not guarantee any tax outcome, deduction amount, or IRS acceptance of your records. You are responsible for reviewing your log and consulting a tax professional when needed.

Retention

  • Local SQLite data remains until you delete it in the app, clear it by wiping data, or remove it by uninstalling (subject to how your OS handles app storage).
  • Optional iCloud backup remains in your private CloudKit data until you remove it.
  • Apple and RevenueCat retain purchase records under their own policies.

How to delete all data

  1. Open Settings
  2. Find Delete all data (near the bottom)
  3. Confirm

That removes trips, vehicles, settings, and related local data from the device. If you used iCloud backup, the wipe flow can also remove the private iCloud copy when you choose that option. Deletion cannot be undone from within MileScope.

Uninstalling the app also removes local app storage on the device. Store subscription billing is separate and is managed in your Apple ID Subscriptions.

Children

MileScope is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app in a way that stored data on a device you control, delete the app data using Settings > Delete all data, or contact the support page.

US privacy notes

MileScope is built for use in the United States. Because trip logs stay on your device (and optionally in your private iCloud), MileScope does not maintain a cloud profile of your drives that you must request from us. You can access, export, and delete your data in the app as described above.

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
